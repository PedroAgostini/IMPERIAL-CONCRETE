Bare concrete

  • SpillsOil and liquids soak in and stain.

Questions Before You Call

How fast will I get an estimate?

Within 24 hours of your request. Send the form or call (913) 995-2657.

Which finish is right for my space?

Epoxy coatings suit high traffic areas such as garages. Quartz is slip resistant and built for outdoor spaces. Marble and metallic finishes bring a seamless, glossy look to interiors. We help you choose during the assessment.

How long does installation take?

It depends on the size and condition of your concrete. We confirm the timeline for your project at the assessment.

When can I use the floor again?

Return to use time varies with the coating and the weather. We confirm it for your project at the assessment.

Do you coat outdoor concrete?

Yes. Quartz coatings are slip resistant and made for patios and pool decks.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. Imperial Concrete Coatings is licensed and insured.
